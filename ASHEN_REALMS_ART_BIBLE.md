# ASHEN REALMS — LOCKED ART DIRECTION & GAME BIBLE

## Core rule
The uploaded Ashen Realms reference image is the visual source of truth for the prototype. New game content must remain visually compatible with it. Do not switch to a different art style.

## Visual language
- Cinematic fantasy realism
- High-detail painted/illustrative environments
- Warm gold/orange sunset light contrasted with deep blue/teal shadows
- Dramatic mountains, waterfalls, rivers, cliffs and distant fantasy architecture
- Noble medieval-fantasy castles and dense living settlements
- Hero silhouettes with detailed armor, cloak and readable weapons
- Premium mobile-game UI: dark translucent panels, gold borders, circular icons, compact resource bars
- Strong depth: foreground rocks/hero, midground city, distant mountains/sky
- Rich but controlled color palette; no flat cartoon/vector look
- Consistent material language: stone, metal, leather, wood, glowing magical accents

## Required game areas represented by the reference
1. Home / castle city
2. Open-world exploration
3. Epic battle
4. Hero progression
5. Inventory / equipment
6. Alliance and territory map
7. NPC dialogue / story
8. Journey / choice events
9. Mobile-first presentation

## Gameplay systems
- Castle and city construction/upgrades
- Resources: food, wood, stone, iron, coins, gold
- Army: infantry, archers, cavalry, siege
- Hero levels, equipment and skills
- World exploration and discovery
- Territory capture and territory merging
- Alliances, alliance wars and rankings
- Quests and branching decisions
- Seasons, tournaments and events
- Cosmetic progression and rewards
- Optional ads for rewards
- Season Pass
- Non-pay-to-win monetization focus

## Camera and presentation
- Mobile portrait/landscape responsive layout
- Isometric/angled kingdom presentation for city
- Third-person exploration presentation
- Cinematic combat framing
- World map overview
- UI must feel embedded in the same fantasy world

## Quality gate
A new asset is accepted only if:
1. It fits the same lighting family.
2. It fits the same medieval-fantasy realism.
3. Its materials and detail density match the reference.
4. Its UI treatment matches the reference.
5. It does not introduce a cartoon, neon, minimalist, sci-fi, or unrelated visual language.

## Development rule
Prototype first with the reference scene as the visual anchor. Then replace individual regions with animated/interactable game assets while preserving composition, palette, lighting and visual identity.
